## Brand
LINK RELOAD / 青蓝档案 is Link's personal archive: open, curious, clear, with blue-white P3R visual rhythm and a custom LR mark. Identify the blog as a personal fan site. Retain the existing reference-media attribution and provenance. Avoid café/day/weather controls, preview labels, dedicated video-play controls. The owner requests music on entry: attempt default playback while respecting browser autoplay restrictions; retain pause and require the first real gesture when automatic audio is blocked.

## Product goals
Give the homepage an opening that moves from a blue wave curtain to white with Memento Mori text, then the one-time falling sequence and continuous underwater loop. Provide a slightly enlarged, independently drawn silver portable player with a cyan LCD, black play/pause end cap, volume and restart controls. Attempt default playback on entry, fetching music only once the browser allows audio, then use a decoded Web Audio buffer for sample-aligned looping. A blocked or pending resume has a bounded wait and shows a first-gesture fallback. First playback and restart begin at the original 0-second opening; after the first 208.106440 seconds, loop back to 6 seconds with a 202.106440-second period. Persist the player through internal blog navigation. Preserve the left-hand character and place desktop copy in the right-hand blue space. Reveal headings, body lines, entries and imagery in layers as they enter view.

Success: desktop video occupies the viewport; portrait media has its own large stage with copy below; the opening never prevents navigation or leaves content hidden after failure; all existing routes, content and management functions work. Non-goals: cloning the official site's commerce, modals, navigation destinations, account systems, game menus or copyrighted logo.

## Personas and jobs
New readers enter the archive and find recent posts or a category. Returning readers can immediately scroll or use navigation. Touch, keyboard, reduced-motion and slow-network visitors have direct access to all content.

## Information architecture
Keep `/`, `/daily/`, `/study/`, `/photos/`, `/play/`, `/search/`, `/about/`, `/admin/`, `/manage/`, all detail URLs and 404. Homepage order remains hero, recent entries, four category cards, about. Preserve collection filtering, search data and content text. The empty gallery remains an honest empty state.

## Design principles
The video is the main image, not a small decorative tile. Keep the character's face and body free of homepage text. Match the source media's left-aligned composition. Opening motion runs once per page visit, then only the underwater scene loops. A scroll, keyboard focus or Escape can leave the opening immediately. Animation enhances content access and never locks scrolling.

## Visual language
Retain existing ocean/cyan/white tokens, italic English display type, clear Chinese headings and numbered bilingual navigation. Desktop hero is 100svh with no solid header bar taking height from the media. The LR badge and navigation sit at the top; primary copy begins at 58% of width in the right blue space. Remove the prior full-image dark overlay and bottom decorative waves that obscured the scene.

Blue curtain color is #029eeb; its wave edge moves down over white. The white text screen uses the original Memento Mori SVG wordmark. Intro and loop share identical sizing and alignment. New motion styles live in `p3r-opening.css`, with existing non-home presentation retained. Title letters rise in a clipped line with 35ms stagger, body lines and post rows enter independently, and imagery uses a retreating white cover. Every reveal is one-time on entry, not tied frame-by-frame to scrolling.

## Components
`ReloadMotion` owns controller startup. `reload.mjs` coordinates blue/quote/fall/ready phases, two video elements, failure paths, navigation, gallery and reveals. `RevealText` renders decorative staggered English letters; meaningful Chinese headings retain normal accessible text. `.hero-media` is an independent media container, used for viewport observation and portrait composition. The opening is decorative, aria-hidden and pointer-transparent. `MusicPlayer` owns the in-flow device beside the homepage introduction, readable LCD, status and show/hide controls; `music-player.mjs` owns the Web Audio graph, decoded buffer, loop, elapsed time, volume and real spectrum. Astro ClientRouter preserves the player node and audio graph between blog pages. Per-page motion/search/toy controllers initialize and dispose through router lifecycle events. Admin and standalone toy destinations keep full document navigation.

## Accessibility
Target WCAG 2.2 AA for modified areas. Preserve skip-to-content, semantic headings, visible focus and keyboard navigation. No flashing. Music defaults to a browser-permitted playback attempt at the owner's request, can be paused, and does not share the silent video controls. When autoplay is blocked, the first real click or key can activate playback; a user pause cancels that intent. No gesture is synthesized to evade browser policy. All audio controls have names, focus indicators and usable touch targets. Spectrum motion respects reduced motion and the global pause preference. No music bytes are requested until the audio context can run; blocked automatic playback waits for a gesture before downloading the track. System reduced motion, saved pause and data saving skip the opening and do not request either video. Autoplay denial and media errors release the opening and preserve the poster. Pausing makes all revealed text/images visible; returning to motion does not replay the opening. A bootstrap watchdog releases the opening if the module fails to load. No-JavaScript content and navigation remain reachable.

## Responsive behavior
Landscape desktop: full-viewport video, object-fit cover, left alignment, copy to the right of the character. At widths ≤900px or portrait aspect ratios: full-viewport 100svh portrait media, using the official 750×1600 portrait source with bottom alignment, all headline and introductory copy in a separate blue region below. Use the supplied landscape source on desktop and official portrait variants on small/portrait screens; do not overlay the main copy on the character. Compact navigation opens above media only when requested. Check 1920×1080, 1280×720, tablet portrait and 390×844; no document-wide horizontal overflow. The main music player is docked in the right-hand space beside the homepage about text; small screens place it below that text. The device and its top buttons are larger and legible; no NIGHT WALK title strip or volume slider is shown. Volume uses the top minus/plus buttons. Show/hide uses the theme blue, cyan offset and angled edges. Hide the complete device and restore it with a music launcher without pausing. Other pages keep only a small lower-right launcher by default; opening it provides playback controls. The persistent root moves to each page outlet without rebuilding the audio graph or stealing focus. The display preference persists; automatic navigation collapse does not overwrite it.

## Interaction states
Blue → quote → fall → ready; the completed intro hands off to the existing loop, holding the final intro image until the loop is available. A bounded timeout, scroll/focus/Escape, pause, reduced motion, hidden/offscreen state or errors can bypass the opening. No permanent loading overlay. A rejected autoplay can retry the loop on a later interaction. Media failure retains the still poster. Search, empty-gallery and admin states keep their existing behavior. Player states are idle (including waiting for an allowed gesture), loading, playing, paused and error; loading can be cancelled, failures can be retried, and a suspended/interrupted audio context requires user resumption. Preserve playback during ordinary internal navigation. A full reload, admin page or standalone toy leaves the current audio document.
